Protects the rights of persons with disabilities and establishes a commission to drive inclusion in education, employment and public life.

Records of the House

Order paper, votes and proceedings

These records are maintained by the Office of the Clerk and are available for inspection at the Legislative Library during working hours.

Order PaperThe business of the House for each sitting day: prayers, approval of votes, announcements, petitions, presentation of reports, bills and motions.
Published the evening before each sitting
Votes and ProceedingsThe official minutes of every sitting, recording attendance, decisions, divisions and referrals.
Laid at the next sitting
HansardThe verbatim record of debate in the chamber.
Compiled by the Hansard department
Committee reportsReports of standing and ad-hoc committees on bills, inquiries and oversight visits.
Laid before the House
Standing OrdersThe rules governing the conduct of business, debate, committees and discipline in the House.
As amended by resolution
Laws of Gombe StateThe compiled laws of the state, including all laws passed by successive Assemblies.
