Leadership of the House

The principal officers elected by members to lead the Seventh Assembly, and the offices that support the work of the House.

Principal officers

Elected to lead the Seventh Assembly

Principal officers are elected by members at the inaugural sitting of each Assembly and hold office at the pleasure of the House.

ML

Rt. Hon. Muhammad Abubakar Luggerewo

Speaker of the House Akko Central APC

Presides over sittings of the House, interprets and enforces the Standing Orders, represents the House in its relations with the executive and the judiciary, and signs every bill passed by the House. Born in March 1984, he holds a Diploma in General Agriculture from Ramat Polytechnic, Maiduguri and a degree in Agricultural Economics from the University of Maiduguri, and worked in banking with Guaranty Trust Bank and Ecobank before entering politics. First elected for Akko Central in 2019, he chaired the Committee on Finance and Economic Planning before being elected Speaker in November 2020 and re-elected to lead the Seventh Assembly in June 2023.

SS

Rt. Hon. Sadam Bello Sale

Deputy Speaker Funakaye North APC

Presides over the House in the absence of the Speaker, supports the leadership of plenary business and chairs the House when it resolves into a Committee of the Whole on matters assigned to him.

LG

Hon. Ladan Yerima Gaule

Majority Leader Kaltungo East APC

Leads government business on the floor, moves the motions that set the order of business, and coordinates the majority caucus in support of legislation before the House.

MB

Hon. Musa Buba

Chief Whip Balanga North APC

Maintains discipline and attendance among members, ensures a quorum is available for sittings and divisions, and communicates the leadership's position on business before the House.

Minority leadership and the Office of the Clerk. Minority caucus officers and the serving Clerk of the House are announced by resolution of the House; the current holders should be confirmed with the Office of the Clerk before publication.

Institutional offices

Offices that keep the House running

Office of the Speaker

Leadership of the House, relations with the other arms of government, and the signing of bills and resolutions.

Office of the Deputy Speaker

Deputises for the Speaker and supports the management of plenary business.

Office of the Clerk of the House

The administrative head of the Assembly's bureaucracy: custody of records, the Votes and Proceedings, the Order Paper, Hansard, petitions and all official correspondence.

Assembly Service Commission

Appointment, discipline and welfare of the legislative service staff who support members and committees.

Serjeant-at-Arms

Custody of the Mace, order within the chamber and precincts, and the security of members and visitors.

Legislative Library & Research

Research briefs for members and committees, the law reports collection, and public access to copies of laws.

Write to the leadership

Correspondence to the Speaker, the Deputy Speaker or any principal officer should be addressed through the Clerk of the House.
