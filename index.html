Welcome from the Speaker

A House that works openly, and in the interest of the people

The Seventh Assembly was inaugurated in June 2023 with a clear charge: to legislate responsibly, appropriate public funds prudently, and exercise oversight without fear or favour. We sit three days a week, our committees work throughout the year, and our public hearings are open to every citizen who wishes to be heard.

“Legislative activities of this House will be conducted with equity, justice and accountability.”

Whether you wish to track a bill, read the Votes and Proceedings, submit a memorandum on legislation before a committee, or simply write to the member representing your constituency, this website is the place to begin.

Constitutional functions

What the Assembly does

The powers of the House flow from Chapter V, Part II of the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999 (as amended), and from the Standing Orders of the House.

Law making

Enacting laws for the peace, order and good government of Gombe State on every matter within the legislative competence of the state.

Appropriation

Scrutinising, amending and passing the annual budget, supplementary estimates and all borrowing or expenditure requests of the executive.

Oversight

Monitoring the implementation of laws and budgets by ministries, departments and agencies through committee inquiries and project inspections.

Representation

Articulating the needs of the twenty-four constituencies and bringing constituents' concerns directly into the chamber.

Confirmation

Screening and confirming nominees for executive and statutory offices submitted by the Governor under the Constitution.

Public petitions

Receiving and investigating petitions from citizens, and recommending redress where a public body has acted wrongly.
