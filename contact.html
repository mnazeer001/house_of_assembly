Attending a sitting

Plenary sittings are open to the public on Tuesdays, Wednesdays & Thursdays, 10:00am. Seating is in the public gallery.

  • Arrive 30 minutes before the sitting
  • Bring a valid means of identification
  • Mobile phones must be silenced
  • Groups of ten or more should apply in advance
  • Photography requires the permission of the Clerk

Questions

Frequently asked

Every one of the 24 state constituencies has an elected member. Find yours on the Members page, note the constituency, then write to the member through the Office of the Clerk at the Assembly Complex or by e-mail. Correspondence addressed to a member by name is forwarded the same working day.

Yes. Plenary sittings are open to the public and are held on Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays from 10:00am. Visitors are seated in the public gallery and should arrive at least thirty minutes early with a valid means of identification. Large groups should apply in advance through the Office of the Clerk.

A petition must be in writing, signed, and addressed to the Speaker through the Clerk. It should state the facts complained of, the relief sought, and must not be in respect of a matter already before a court. Petitions are referred to the Committee on Judiciary, Justice and Public Petitions, which may invite the parties to a hearing.

When a bill passes second reading it is referred to a committee, which ordinarily calls for memoranda and holds a public hearing. Notices of hearings are published on the News page and in the state media. Written memoranda may be submitted to the Clerk before the advertised deadline.

The Votes and Proceedings of each sitting, the Order Paper, Hansard and copies of laws passed by the House are available from the Legislative Library and the Office of the Clerk during working hours. Certified true copies attract the prescribed fee.

Yes. The Assembly runs a legislative internship for students of law, political science and public administration, and hosts guided chamber tours for schools on non-sitting days. Applications go to the Office of the Clerk.
