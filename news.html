News, notices and press releases

Decisions of the House, committee activity, public hearing notices and announcements from the Office of the Clerk.

Notice22 September 2026

Public notice: memoranda invited on pending bills

The Office of the Clerk invites written memoranda from the public, professional bodies and civil society on all bills at committee stage. Submissions may be delivered to the Clerk's office or sent by e-mail.

Press release
Notice8 September 2026

Legislative internship and chamber tours resume for schools

Secondary schools and tertiary institutions may apply through the Office of the Clerk for guided tours of the chamber and a briefing on the law-making process. Visits take place on non-sitting days.

Press release
Plenary16 July 2026

House confirms 23 commissioner-nominees after screening

Following a two-week screening exercise, the House confirmed the nominees forwarded by His Excellency under Section 192(2) of the 1999 Constitution (as amended). Nominees appeared before the Committee of the Whole and responded to questions on their portfolios, assets and plans.

Press release
Plenary2 July 2026

Governor transmits list of 23 commissioner-nominees to the Assembly

The Speaker read a letter dated 29 June from Governor Muhammadu Inuwa Yahaya requesting the Assembly's approval to appoint members of the State Executive Council, after which the list was referred for screening.

Press release
Oversight5 January 2026

Committees begin first-quarter oversight of capital projects

Standing committees commenced inspection visits to health, education and road projects captured in the 2026 Appropriation Law, with findings to be reported to the House at the resumption of plenary.

Press release
Legislation29 December 2025

Governor assents to the ₦617.95bn 2026 Appropriation Law

The Speaker led members to the Council Chamber to present the Appropriation Bill for assent. The Assembly reviewed the executive proposal of ₦535.69bn upward by over ₦82bn, with ₦428.5bn earmarked for capital expenditure.

Press release
Legislation15 December 2025

House passes 2026 budget after clause-by-clause consideration

The Appropriation Committee laid its report after budget-defence sessions with ministries, departments and agencies and an extended working session. The House resolved into a Committee of the Whole before the third reading.

Press release
Public hearing16 August 2025

Public hearing held on the creation of 13 new council development areas

Traditional rulers, civil society organisations and community representatives submitted memoranda on the bill. The Speaker explained that the House expedited the first and second readings because of the bill's importance to grassroots development.

Press release
Announcement20 November 2024

Groundbreaking for the new Assembly Complex at the Three-Arms Zone

The new legislative complex, part of a ₦28.9bn twin project with the State High Court complex, will house the chamber, committee rooms, the legislative library and members' offices in a single secure precinct.

Press release
Legislation27 October 2024

Assembly passes bill establishing the State Disability Commission

The private member's bill sponsored by Hon. Shuaibu Adamu Haruna was passed after the Committee on Women Affairs and Social Development reported amendments to 23 sections arising from the public hearing.
