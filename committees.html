Standing committees

The committees of the House examine bills clause by clause, hold public hearings and carry out oversight of ministries, departments and agencies.

Where the detailed work happens

Eighteen standing committees

Committees are constituted at the start of each Assembly. Each has a chairman, a deputy chairman and members drawn from both the majority and the minority caucus.

01

Appropriation

Scrutinises the annual budget estimates, conducts budget-defence sessions with every MDA and reports the Appropriation Bill to the House.

Chairman: Hon. Aliyu Baba Manu

02

Finance & Economic Planning

Oversees revenue generation, internally generated revenue performance, public debt and the state's medium-term expenditure framework.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

03

Public Accounts

Examines the Auditor-General's reports and holds accounting officers to account for public expenditure.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

04

Works, Housing & Transport

Oversight of road construction and rehabilitation, public buildings, urban transport and the state's infrastructure contracts.

Chairman: Hon. Mustapha Usman Hassan

05

Education, Science & Technology

Covers basic and secondary education, tertiary institutions, teacher welfare, school infrastructure and the ICT agenda of the state.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

06

Health & Human Services

Primary health care, general hospitals, the state specialist hospital, drug management agency and health insurance scheme.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

07

Agriculture & Animal Husbandry

Fertiliser distribution, irrigation, livestock development, grazing reserves and farmer–herder peace frameworks.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

08

Environment & Forest Resources

Afforestation, erosion control, sanitation agencies, climate resilience programmes and environmental impact compliance.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

09

Water Resources

Urban and rural water supply schemes, boreholes, dams and the state water board.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

10

Women Affairs, Social Development & Special Duties

Gender legislation, child rights, persons with disabilities, social investment programmes and vulnerable-group protection.

Chairman: Hon. Gabriel Galadima Fushison

11

Local Government & Chieftaincy Affairs

Local government administration and finances, the new local council development areas, and traditional institutions.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

12

Judiciary, Justice & Public Petitions

Administration of justice, the state judiciary's autonomy and the hearing of petitions brought by members of the public.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

13

Security & Special Services

Internal security, community policing structures, vigilance groups and the state's contribution to regional security funds.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

14

Information, Culture & Tourism

State media organisations, public enlightenment, cultural heritage sites and the tourism potential of the state.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

15

Youth & Sports Development

Youth empowerment schemes, sports facilities and the state's participation in national competitions.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

16

Commerce, Industry & Investment

Markets, the industrial park, small and medium enterprises, and the state's investment promotion framework.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

17

Rules & Business

Regulates the order of business of the House, sitting arrangements and the Standing Orders.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

18

Ethics, Privileges & Public Service Matters

Conduct of members, legislative privileges, civil service welfare and pension administration.

Chairman announced by resolution of the House

Committee powers

  • Summon any person to give evidence
  • Require the production of documents
  • Conduct public hearings on bills
  • Inspect projects and facilities
  • Examine the accounts of MDAs
  • Report findings and recommendations to the House

Appear before a committee

Written memoranda on any bill at committee stage may be submitted to the Clerk. Include your name, address, telephone number and the title of the bill.

Ad-hoc committees

In addition to the standing committees, the House constitutes ad-hoc committees for specific inquiries and for the screening of nominees. They dissolve once their report is laid.

Oversight in practice

Oversight is not a formality. Committees visit project sites, compare what was appropriated with what has been delivered, and invite accounting officers to explain variances. Findings are laid before the House and may lead to resolutions, further inquiry or a referral to the Public Accounts Committee.

In the first quarter of each year, committees inspect the capital projects captured in the Appropriation Law and report before the House considers the next budget cycle.
